## Research focus

The note adapts the Boltzmann–Arrhenius–Zhurkov model to ionizing-radiation exposure of electronics. It uses a radiation-dependent term and a leakage-current failure criterion to relate accelerated testing to lifetime prediction. The paper presents a modeling approach and numerical example; it explicitly identifies experimental validation as future work.

# Publication catalog — Artem L. Ponomarev

[Repository overview](README.md)

This catalog brings together **33 identified journal articles**, three conference/proceedings papers or chapters, three NASA technical reports, two NASA Tech Briefs, and a doctoral dissertation. Conference abstracts and posters are cataloged separately. Historical manuscript titles are retained below without counting them as additional published articles.

**Scope:** space radiation, stochastic modeling, chromosome aberrations, scientific computing, image analysis, neuroscience, polymer physics, plasma physics, and electronics/software-system reliability. A publication appearing after my NASA engagement is not necessarily work performed while employed there.

**Reading the records:** bold author names identify me, not sole authorship. Publisher, PubMed, NASA, and institutional records are linked where identified. Entries explicitly marked **CV-derived** retain bibliographic information recovered from my supplied curriculum vitae; missing identifiers and unresolved metadata are not invented. This is an expanded, deduplicated catalog—not a claim that every career publication or conference appearance has been recovered.

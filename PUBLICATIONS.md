# Publication catalog — Artem L. Ponomarev

[Repository overview](README.md) · [Individual access guides](publications/README.md) · [Public research CV](cv/Artem_Ponomarev_Public_Research_CV.md)

This catalog contains **33 journal-article records**, **3 proceedings papers/chapters**, **3 NASA technical reports**, **2 NASA Tech Briefs**, and **1 doctoral dissertation**. Conference appearances are in [PRESENTATIONS.md](PRESENTATIONS.md). Historical manuscript titles below are not counted as additional published articles.

Scope: space radiation, stochastic modeling, chromosome aberrations, scientific computing, image analysis, neuroscience, polymer physics, plasma physics, and reliability engineering. Publication after an employment period does not imply continued employment at that institution.

Bold author names identify Artem Ponomarev, not sole authorship. **CV-derived** entries retain author-supplied metadata where a publisher identifier or final detail remains unresolved. Missing identifiers are not invented. This is an expanded research catalog, not a certified exhaustive career total.

## Research profile

Computational physicist and scientific-software researcher with work in space-radiation transport and biological effects, stochastic chromosome and DNA-damage modeling, image analysis, polymer physics, and reliability engineering. Publications connect analytical and Monte Carlo methods with experimental interpretation and software implementation.

## Research methods and software

Monte Carlo simulation; stochastic processes; chromosome and chromatin geometry; DNA-damage and repair kinetics; radiation transport and dosimetry; numerical model–experiment comparison; microscopy image segmentation; scientific visualization; ray tracing and CAD-based shielding analysis; Linux-based numerical computing and parallel workflows.

Related software names include **NASARTI**, **RITCARD**, **RITRACKS**, and **GERM**. These are distinct systems, and contribution to a coauthored publication does not imply sole ownership of every component. [Companion C# archive](https://github.com/artemponomarevjetski/NASA-csharp-reinforcement-learning-for-space-particles).

# Conference abstracts, posters, and presentations

[Repository overview](README.md) · [Publication catalog](PUBLICATIONS.md)

This register separates conference communication from journal publication. A poster, talk, software demonstration, and later journal paper can describe related research without representing multiple journal articles. Author lists are preserved when available; being a coauthor does not establish who personally delivered a presentation.

The six 2018–2019 records below are listed in the bibliography of NASA's [FY2019 Task Book report, task 12380](https://taskbook.nasaprs.com/tbp/tbpdf.cfm?id=12380), pages 2–3. ### P01 · Computer visualization of the experimental count of chromosomal aberrations

**Ponomarev AL**, Plante I, Patel ZS, Slaba T, Hada M.  
**2019 NASA Human Research Program Investigators' Workshop**, Galveston, Texas, January 22–25, 2019. Abstract/poster.  
[Archived poster](PosterHRP_IWS2019_ArtemPonomarev.pdf) · [NASA Task Book bibliography](https://taskbook.nasaprs.com/tbp/tbpdf.cfm?id=12380)

## Attribution boundary

The bookmarked [2020 bi-exponential-repair poster](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20200001304) lists Plante, Patel, Slaba, and Hada as authors. It cites the coauthored RITCARD paper and Ponomarev–Sachs chromatin-model work, but **does not list Ponomarev as an author**. It belongs in related research, not in this authored-presentation register.

The same author-by-author check applies to every paper in a project bibliography: membership in a research team or appearance in a reference list does not by itself establish coauthorship.

Register consolidated **November 17, 2026** from NASA records, the archived poster, and the author-supplied CV. Historical CV entries remain labeled until independently reconciled.
